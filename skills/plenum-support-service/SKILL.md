# plenum support service

**Apply when:** Support workflows, inbox triage and founder communications preparation.

## Procedure
1. Check the actual user issue, product facts, service policy and source dates.
2. Resolve the question plainly; do not promise refunds or policy exceptions not established.
3. Draft precise communications that respect privacy and customer identity.
4. Identify escalations, promised deadlines and policy/legal questions; maintain an evidence and revision trail.
5. Never send messages or view customer accounts without explicit permissions.

## Required deliverable
Issue | Verified facts | Proposed answer | Required escalation | Founder approval.
