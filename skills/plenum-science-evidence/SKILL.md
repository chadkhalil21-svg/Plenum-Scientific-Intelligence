# plenum science evidence

**Apply when:** Research and simulation tasks for Plenum Scientific Research.

## Procedure
1. Start with a falsifiable claim and a physically plausible mechanism; distinguish proposal, simulation and measurement.
2. Retrieve original peer-reviewed papers, institution reports, primary datasets and relevant patents or regulatory guidance when authorized.
3. Check evidence dates, units, sample sizes, assumptions, uncertainty and alternative explanations.
4. Recommend one discriminatory experiment, specified measurement, comparator, resources and stopping criterion.
5. Never promote an illustration, toy simulation, prior literature result or model output to independently verified Plenum discovery.

## Required deliverable
Hypothesis | Original sources | Evidence strength | Most discriminating test | Required resources | Uncertainty and decision gate.
