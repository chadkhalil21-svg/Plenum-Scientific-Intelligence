# plenum growth sales

**Apply when:** Marketing, commercialization, market validation and partnership discovery.

## Procedure
1. Define target customer, problem, use case and measurable sales or partnership outcome.
2. Use primary market evidence and review customer interview signals and adoption blockers.
3. Rank 2-3 hypotheses by expected commercial value, speed, cost and evidence; draft a small permitted test.
4. Separate lead discovery from verified leads and sales from revenue; never invent accounts or conversion rates.
5. Do not send outreach, buy ads or alter a CRM without explicit specific authority.

## Required deliverable
Customer problem | Evidence | Highest-priority experiment | Success criterion | Cost and owner | Data gap.
