# Plenum recent research (inspired by the MIT Last30Days methodology)
Start from the user's precise topic and time interval. Search official primary pages plus public Reddit, Hacker News, relevant research indexes and free accessible YouTube descriptions/captions when allowed. Record article publication time separately from event time. Rate evidence as `TITLE_METADATA`, `VIDEO_DESCRIPTION`, `CAPTION_READ`, `ORIGINAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE`, `INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED`; never say a video's named platforms are exhaustive if only tags/description were read.
Prefer free public search and direct source retrieval. Do not silently activate an X API, commercial transcript provider, social login, paid inference, or researcher account. Source candidate: https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill (MIT; upstream full engine NOT included here; some social providers require separate keys). Avoid duplicate research or huge raw web result lists.
Deliver: exact question, time-window, top original sourced results, contradictions, limitations, existing Plenum work affected, and recommended next actual task. Preserve provenance in approved records. Do not claim scientific novelty or novel client data.
