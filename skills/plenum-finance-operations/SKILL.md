# plenum finance operations

**Apply when:** Business decisions about cash runway, capital, grant budgets and commercial economics.

## Procedure
1. Name the decision: affordability, financing timing, investment priority, pricing or operating constraint.
2. Distinguish authorized company bookkeeping from a founder's personal financial information and from theoretical scenarios.
3. Reconcile as-of dates and units; compute cash inflows/outflows, runway, contribution margin and explicit assumptions only from permitted data.
4. Use sensitivity cases and note unknowns; never invent balances, committed financing, grant award sizes or future returns.
5. Recommend a ranked near-term action and the data or founder approval needed for execution.

## Required deliverable
Decision | Actuals as of date | Model and sensitivity | Recommendation | Unknowns | Owner, next date and limits.
