# plenum simulation validity

**Apply when:** Scientific simulation of known models and engineering estimates.

## Procedure
1. Specify governing assumptions, units, boundary conditions and where underlying physics is supported.
2. Use existing tested numerical tools before importing a new package.
3. Document model inputs and provenance, compare to analytic or empirical baseline and quantify error.
4. Provide sensitivity analysis and limitations; prohibit extrapolation beyond validated regime.
5. Do not confuse a known-model simulation or generated chart with a measured new physical effect.

## Required deliverable
Model | Baseline | Inputs and assumptions | Results | Sensitivity | Validation boundary.
