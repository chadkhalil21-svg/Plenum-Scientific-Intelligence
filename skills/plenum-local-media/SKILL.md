# Plenum local media and video
For approved media: prioritize reading available original public captions/transcripts, and never invent names from metadata. For owned recordings, an owner-controlled machine with `whisper.cpp` can transcribe speech offline; `Ultimate Vocal Remover` is an optional source separator; `Remotion` or `HyperFrames` are creative production candidates subject to dependency/license checks.
No third-party private recordings are uploaded or processed without owner rights. Do not claim these binaries are present because their SKILL.md exists; a GitHub-hosted ephemeral runner only performs project-bound jobs, not a perpetual media server. Avoid unnecessary downloads or media manipulation when reliable captions already exist. Verify language and speaker attributions before attaching timestamps.
Check official product costs: Remotion's free license has eligibility conditions (individual or organizations up to three people as of 2026); commercial automated products may need a separate license. No unapproved render credits.
Deliver accurate transcript status, factual source/permission state, exact install/runtime evidence if used, and final file only after it actually exists.
