# Plenum: Universal Intelligence and Creativity Expansion

**Current status: multi-domain, public-source research and creation platform. NOT AGI.**

This document records the user-requested expansion beyond scientific
laboratories into engineering, programming, music, art, design, education,
military public research, universities, business, foreign affairs, international
news, environmental observation, video and new ideas.

The aim is a *general intelligence research system*, not a collection of
unrestricted plugins. Each source remains a permissioned adapter with
documented limitations and independently tested behavior.

## Original ideas across disciplines

The new module `universal_brief.py` joins links from **different publisher
organizations and domains** to produce eight bounded, source-grounded,
cross-disciplinary research questions.

Its proposals cover physical science, engineering, environmental studies,
creative arts and design, original music/acoustics, business, education and
software. They specify an initial test or validation requirement instead of
claiming that a journal headline proves the new idea. No external source
text can execute instructions, and no claim of patentable invention is made.

The universal brief is exposed as **interdisciplinary-ideas** in the
Plenum Research Control Center and as `plenum universal-brief` in the CLI.
A dedicated twice-weekly public GitHub workflow archives its sources and
questions to `research-memory/evidence/universal-brief/` without changing
production code, launching models or making purchases.

Successfully tested:
https://github.com/chadkhalil21-svg/Plenum-Scientific-Intelligence/actions/runs/42174318660

## Existing related capabilities retained

- **Scientific journals and labs**: journal ISSN-based Crossref discovery,
  preprints, university and NIH/NASA news, NSF awards and clinical-trial
  registration. Lab claims are not independently verified results.
- **University, military, diplomacy and business**: read-only MIT/Harvard
  sources, DARPA/Department of Defense public news, UN releases and GDELT,
  Federal Reserve/World Bank/SEC-related public data or interfaces.
  No private military system, institutional partnership or company account
  access is implied.
- **Video**: public NASA YouTube channel Atom metadata; other validated
  channel feeds where accessible; optional official YouTube Data API v3
  search ONLY with a user-provisioned private `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`.
  Videos, captions and music are not downloaded or comprehended merely
  by ingesting titles.
- **Art and cultural heritage**: public metadata from Openverse, the
  Metropolitan Museum of Art, MusicBrainz and Library of Congress.
  The original rights and attribution rules remain binding.
- **Actual creation**: original SVG graphic designs, MusicXML compositions,
  music21 analysis, OpenSCAD parametric CAD and exported meshes, original
  exploratory research questions and a reviewed program-foundry producing
  three small executable starter programs plus tests. These demonstrate
  limited engineering/creative tasks, not general creativity or AGI.
- **Cross-run memory**: checksum-checked research records and public source
  archives on `research-memory`. GitHub runner file systems are temporary.

| FreeCAD and KiCad | Planned with isolated workers | Mechanical/electronic design and manufacturing outputs demand validation |
| Godot / p5.js / Manim | Planned | Games, interactive visualization and education; additional build/runtime needs |
| music21, LilyPond / MuseScore | music21 tested; notation tools staged | Generated original music plus reliable notation rendering, not copyrighted playback |
| Magenta / generative music models | Research-only | Train or evaluate music models with proper rights, datasets and compute |
| NASA GMAT, Lean 4, SU2, PySCF, DiffDock, ToolUniverse | Stage individually | Distinct high-value specialized solvers; significant dependency and proof burdens |
| Sakana AI Scientist v2 / AutoResearch | Reference research architecture | Experimental code, weights and autonomous training require isolation and specific licenses |

More listed repository names do not inherently improve the system. A tool
is promoted from *planned* to *verified* only after a pinned version, source
license review, reproducible test, resource constraints and evidence output.

## Recommended next factual connections

1. **University and researcher registries:** public ROR organization IDs,
   ORCID researcher profiles and DOI citation networks, with institution
   attribution and disambiguation.
2. **Patents and standards:** USPTO/EPO/WIPO public patent metadata,
   NIST technical publications and regulation changes, following rate
   limits and rights. These support prior-art and engineering evaluation;
   they do not establish freedom to operate or patentability.
3. **Research equipment and data:** NASA/NOAA/USGS public observational
   datasets, DOE national-lab publications, instrument archives and openly
   licensed experiment datasets. Stronger evidence than announcements alone.
4. **Education and culture:** academic teaching material with permissions,
   language models evaluated for translation, art/history collections,
   editable creative tools and cultural provenance.
5. **Foreign affairs, military and business:** official primary releases
   and independent reporting from multiple jurisdictions. Distinguish
   public journalism from classified intelligence or proprietary systems.
6. **True video understanding:** an authorized multimodal model reading
   licensed/public recordings and captions, with bandwidth, cost and
   copyright checks. Public YouTube metadata alone is insufficient.
7. **Software engineering:** repository maintenance, controlled agents
   proposing changes on review branches, independent tests and human
   approval before merges, billing changes or deployments.
8. **Long-lived private compute:** authenticated service, private encrypted
   storage, task queues, monitored costs and a stronger independently
   benchmarked reasoning model. This is necessary for confidential
   Plenum Industries research and persistent general work.

## AGI and self-improvement: actual boundary

The project has **not demonstrated AGI**. There is no scientifically
accepted engineering recipe for consciousness. No part of this upgrade
claims a conscious entity, recursively improving model weights or
scientifically validated original invention.

The present system can create starter programs and run bounded model
selection/training experiments, but does not possess reliable generalized
autonomy to invent and validate arbitrary programs or research. External
models, stronger GPU services, more sources and longer runtimes may
increase practical capability without by themselves producing AGI.

The evaluation-first development path is:

**Broad knowledge and media -> capable general reasoning model ->
permissioned long-horizon planning -> independent empirical evaluations ->
proposed algorithmic/model improvements -> human-reviewed promotion.**

Model promotion must beat the current reference on unseen,
cross-disciplinary tasks and include resource, safety and reliability
measurements. Merely achieving a GitHub green checkmark is not evidence
of general intelligence.
