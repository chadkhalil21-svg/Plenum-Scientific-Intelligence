# Terra five-minute documentary — scene timing acceptance gate (14 November 2026)

**Status:** Editorial acceptance criterion only; not a rendered film, narration audition, or scientific/rights approval. Companion to the existing director script, timing audit and rights gate; do not repeat completed short-render proofs.

## Deterministic duration constraint
The ten scenes each have a nominal 30-second window. The script labels their spoken word counts 57, 53, 57, 58, 61, 57, 61, 65, 63, 58 (total **590**). At 118 spoken words/minute, 590 words consume exactly **300 seconds**, leaving zero time for any title hold, silence, dramatic pause, or instrumental ending. Even 120 wpm consumes 295 seconds, leaving only five seconds across the entire film. The 30-second scene marks are therefore **targets, not verified synchronized edit points**.

## Actual edit acceptance procedure (no new service)
1. Retain the existing ten-scene public script and scientific classifications; do not replace source-backed caveats with promotional claims. For every scene, record **actual narrated WAV start/end**, spoken-word count, and available visual duration after a human voice audition. No fixed-rate estimate can certify audio fit.
2. Reserve at least 2 seconds per scene for meaningful visual breathing/transition as an **editorial planning allowance**, then audition; this gives <=280 seconds for speech. At 118 wpm the corresponding rough upper bound is **551 words**. A more cinematic 30 seconds of total pauses/transitions gives <=270 seconds speech and about **531 words**. These are planning ceilings, not mandated cuts or measured performance.
3. Revise only the minimum narration necessary to fit the accepted voice, preserving attribution to published *Asparagopsis* supplementation studies, and explicitly distinguish Plenum Terra's unvalidated engineered-forage concept. Do not imply measured Terra efficacy, animal safety, regulatory approval, or actual experimental footage.
4. Generate/align captions from the **final accepted narration**, not from planned scene marks. Require every caption cue to have nonnegative start, end > start, monotonic timing, no unintended overlap, and coverage of spoken words; visually inspect first/middle/last cues and listen through transitions.
5. Verify real full-duration MP4 via independent decode and playback, actual stream duration, H.264/AAC streams, synchronized audio and captions, first/middle/last frame inspection, rights-cleared exact footage, and human review of narrator naturalness. **NOT TESTED** until actual media exists.

**Release boundary:** This gate does not approve any footage, score, voice model/checkpoint, or publication. No paid API, trial, hosted rendering inference, secret, or private patent data is required.
