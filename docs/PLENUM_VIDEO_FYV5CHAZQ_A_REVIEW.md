## Recommendations distilled from the whole chapter-by-chapter outline
- **One shared tool per function, not all ten installations.** Ollama is our current bounded free inference; LM Studio duplicates its local-model job. Existing ChatGPT/web research can cover much of Perplexity. NotebookLM complements source-grounded paper reading rather than serving agents.
- **Incorporate the genuinely distinct free abilities first:** browser-based Napkin AI for occasional professional figures; Upscayl only on a compatible owner GPU computer; `whisper.cpp` only for authorized owner audio and actual installed model weights; public Hugging Face Spaces to shortlist existing ML packages.
- **Test provider tiers only if a real unserved need appears.** Google AI Studio has meaningful free models, but the free tier's potential model-improvement use of prompts makes public nonsensitive tasks the default. A free demo with sleep or account limits is NOT a free 24/7 private service.
- **Inspect sources before presenting technical results.** Upscaling alters image details; source-grounded AI can still misread or hallucinate; verified primary sources remain the Goldfish acceptance standard.
- **Do not substitute an extra agent for completion auditing.** Existing Chief, verification reviewer and `docs/PLENUM_FOUNDER_INSTRUCTION_COVERAGE.json` already divide responsibilities; video-specific source retrieval and an actual verified output are more valuable than a new coordinator.
