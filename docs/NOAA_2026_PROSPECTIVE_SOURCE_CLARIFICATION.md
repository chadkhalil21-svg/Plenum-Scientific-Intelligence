# NOAA 2026 prospective study: public observation-source clarification (PRE-ISSUANCE)

**Recorded:** November 9, 2026, **before** the November 10 precommitted forecast issue and November 12–13 test observations. **Status:** method/source clarification, **not** an issued forecast, test result, post-hoc prediction or independent scientific advance.

## Why this adjustment is necessary

The original prospective research protocol [`protocols/noaa-prospective-2026-11.json`](../protocols/noaa-prospective-2026-11.json) was committed November 7, 2026 (original protocol commit `45ac814869b9e47450c00ec55e83b29f02d3ca71`). Its source adapter originally reused `product=hourly_height` from **older verified** NOAA experimental examples. NOAA's original documented API states `hourly_height` is **verified hourly** observations, normally available only after a station's **monthly review**, whereas `water_level` offers preliminary **or** verified 6-minute observations. A November 12–13, 2026 study audited November 16 cannot depend on future monthly-verified hourly data that NOAA does not promise to publish by then. This is a **source availability error**, not new model performance evidence. Sources:

- Original [NOAA CO-OPS Data API specification](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/), *Data Products*: `water_level` = preliminary/verified six-minute observed water levels, `hourly_height` = verified hourly; monthly timing warning. The six-minute product supports queries up to **one month**.
- Original [NOAA CO-OPS service information](https://www.co-ops.nos.noaa.gov/web_services_info.html), real observation products and limits.
- Verified monthly data is scientifically different from preliminary hourly subsampling. They must **not** be silently equated.

## Single explicitly declared observation source for BOTH forecast development and future evaluation

- **Data origin:** NOAA CO-OPS `https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/datagetter`. Actual observed `product=water_level` (six-minute) only; **never** the separately model-predicted `predictions` product or an opportunistic mix of verified and preliminary APIs.
- **Precommitted stations:** Boston `8443970` and Virginia Key `8723214`. **Unchanged.**
- **Dates:** original October 24–November 8 UTC development, future November 12–13 UTC observations, source query at audit may include intermediate days but model-fit cannot read them. **Unchanged.**
- **Sampling:** select ONLY actual `hh:00` GMT/UTC *measured* observations from NOAA's six-minute records, with exact timestamp, no average, interpolation, backfill or synthetic observations. All original forecast models, folds and MAE still operate on **one recorded height at each UTC hour**. This series is **NOT NOAA's derived `hourly_height` product**.
- **Status:** require NOAA `q=p` (preliminary) or `q=v` (verified), a parseable four-part original NOAA `f` flag, and valid real finite height. Reject flat/rate/limit flagged observations (flag positions 2–4), inferred verified readings (first flag for `q=v`), missing flags or invalid values. Preliminary first flag is the NOAA *count* of outlying one-second samples, not itself a binary rejection flag. Missing or anomalous top-of-hour readings remain **missing**. Do not replace them with adjacent six-minute readings or estimated tides. Each selected observation remains MLLW datum, metric meters and GMT time. The test may legitimately fail if enough observations are unavailable. See [NOAA original response-field help](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/responseHelp.html) for the `q` and `f` meanings.
- **Scope of API call:** October 24–November 8 (development) or October 24–November 13 (**auditor only after original audit date gate**): respectively 16 and 21 days of source, both within NOAA's one-month six-minute maximum. An explicit **1,200,000-byte response cap** replaces the 240 kB originally sized for verified hourly observations. Original raw-response SHA256 and derived hourly-series digest remain independently reproducible and included in the sealed model proposals. No unlimited downloads.
- **Revisions:** NOAA preliminary readings can be corrected later. Preserve originally sealed development hashes; the auditor must reject changed development readings rather than silently refit, replace, or cherry-pick models. A corresponding no-score due to revisions must be reported as a **data source/prospectivity integrity failure**, not zero skill or a successful prediction.

**What is NOT changed:** The November 7 immutable protocol document, source protocol commit identifier, target days, original two Qwen/SmolLM2 frozen checkpoints, fixed forecast equations, candidate bounds, rolling training folds, comparison with three original baselines, minimum selection improvement, autonomous deployment prohibition, pre-future forecast deadline, November 16 independent audit and failure-retention policy. No future-target observations were fetched, scored or used to make this pre-issuance change.

This clarification is committed separately in GitHub's public version history **before** the model issuance window, rather than silently rewriting the earlier research protocol. The original program source and all future original `source_commit` fields identify which observation-series extraction actually ran. It may affect numerical comparability with earlier **verified** hourly_height experiments. Do **not** claim a directly identical measured outcome across the two products.
